  "I don't even know what this app does yet, why would I let it bug me?"

00:01:05 Elena Rossi: So my recommendation is that we don't ask during onboarding at all. We ask the first time someone gets assigned a job, with a short explanation like "get notified when you're assigned new work."

00:01:22 Elena Rossi: I checked with Marcus this morning, he said it's actually simpler for them.
